SLIDE 2: RESEARCH OBJECTIVE
---------------------------
Primary Question: "How do different market regimes affect prediction performance across financial instruments, and which machine learning techniques most effectively leverage cross-market signals to predict price movements in varying market conditions?"

Key Goals:
1. Develop regime detection framework for financial markets
2. Implement cross-market feature engineering
3. Train regime-specific machine learning models
4. Deploy cloud-based real-time prediction system

================================================================

SLIDE 3: DATASET OVERVIEW
--------------------------
Three Financial Market Datasets:
1. S&P 500 Stocks: 50,000 daily records from 500+ stocks (2019-2024)
2. Cryptocurrencies: 50,000 price records (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Dogecoin)
3. ETFs: 50,000 price records from various ETF instruments

Standardized Features: Date, Open, High, Low, Close, Volume, Symbol
Total Records: ~150,000 across all datasets

================================================================

SLIDE 4: NOVEL REGIME DETECTION FRAMEWORK
------------------------------------------
Three-Dimensional Regime Classification:

1. VOLATILITY REGIMES
   - Low Volatility: Stable market conditions
   - Medium Volatility: Normal trading conditions  
   - High Volatility: Market stress periods

2. TREND REGIMES
   - Bull Market: Positive price trends
   - Bear Market: Negative price trends
   - Sideways: Range-bound markets

3. CORRELATION REGIMES
   - High Correlation: Markets moving together
   - Low Correlation: Independent market movements
   - Dynamic Correlation: Changing relationships

SLIDE 6: MACHINE LEARNING MODELS
---------------------------------
Three Distinct Approaches:

1. ARIMA WITH REGIME SWITCHING
   - Traditional statistical approach
   - Regime-specific parameters
   - Markov switching capabilities
   - Mathematical formulation: y_t = μ_s_t + Σφ_i,s_t y_t-i + Σθ_j,s_t ε_t-j + ε_t

2. XGBOOST WITH CROSS-MARKET FEATURES
   - Gradient boosting algorithm
   - Cross-market feature integration
   - Regime-aware training
   - Hyperparameters: 100 estimators, max_depth=6, learning_rate=0.1

3. RANDOM FOREST WITH REGIME AWARENESS
   - Ensemble method
   - Regime-specific models
   - Feature importance analysis
   - Hyperparameters: 100 estimators, max_depth=10

SLIDE 9: CROSS-MARKET ANALYSIS INSIGHTS
----------------------------------------
KEY DISCOVERIES:

VOLATILITY SPILLOVERS:
- Cryptocurrency volatility shows strong spillover effects to traditional markets
- High-stress periods create cross-market volatility transmission
- Regime transitions amplify spillover effects

LEAD-LAG RELATIONSHIPS:
- ETF movements often lead stock market movements by 1-2 days
- Cryptocurrency movements show delayed correlation with traditional markets
- Cross-market timing relationships vary by regime

CORRELATION DYNAMICS:
- Cross-market correlations vary significantly across regimes
- Highest correlations during high-volatility regimes
- Correlation breakdown during regime transitions
